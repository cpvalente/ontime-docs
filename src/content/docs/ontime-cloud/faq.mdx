## Common questions

### What is the difference between Ontime Cloud and self-hosting Ontime?

Ontime Cloud runs the server for you and gives each stage a stable HTTPS link. It also adds read-only share links, archives, the Overview and a team dashboard. Running Ontime yourself is free and supports OSC and automation to devices on your local network. \
See [Ontime Cloud vs the desktop app and self-hosting](/ontime-cloud/#ontime-cloud-vs-the-desktop-app-and-self-hosting).

### How much does Ontime Cloud cost, and is there a free trial?

Ontime itself is free and open source. Ontime Cloud is a paid subscription with PRO and STUDIO plans, with no per-seat pricing. There is no free trial, but you can [ask for a demo](mailto:mail@getontime.no). \
See the [pricing page](https://www.getontime.no/pricing) and the [plan limits](/ontime-cloud/team-and-account/#plans).

### Can I move my project from the desktop app to Ontime Cloud?

Yes. Cloud uses the same project file format. Export the project from local Ontime and upload it to a running stage, or download it from Cloud and import it locally. \
See [Move a project between local and Cloud](/ontime-cloud/features/#move-a-project-between-local-and-cloud).

### How do I share a rundown with someone who has no account?

Create a [share link](/ontime-cloud/share-links/). Viewers open it in any browser without logging in, and only see a read-only rundown or timeline. You can add a password and an expiry.

### Does Ontime Cloud support OSC or Bitfocus Companion?

[Bitfocus Companion](/additional-notes/companion-module/), the [HTTP and WebSocket API](/api/) and the [MCP server](/features/mcp-server/) work with Cloud: point them at your stage link. OSC, including OSC automation outputs, is not available in Cloud.

### Can I run several rooms or stages at once?

Yes. PRO includes 3 stages and STUDIO includes 10. The [Overview](/ontime-cloud/overview/) shows all your running stages on one timeline, and you can share it with a single link.

### What happens to my stages if my subscription ends?

Your stages are stopped, not deleted. Archives and share links are kept. Subscribe again and start your stages: they come back on the same links. \
See [When a subscription ends](/ontime-cloud/features/#when-a-subscription-ends).

### Does everyone who uses a stage need an account?

No. Only people who manage stages in the dashboard need an [invite](/ontime-cloud/team-and-account/#who-needs-an-invite). Operators can open the stage link, and viewers can use a share link.

### Is my show backed up?

Create an [archive](/ontime-cloud/features/#archives) before big changes, between show days or after the event. Archives stay in Cloud even if you delete the stage. You can also [download the project](/ontime-cloud/features/#download-a-project) at any time.

### Can I get a custom domain?

STUDIO customers can ask for extensions such as custom domains or vanity URLs. See [Plans](/ontime-cloud/team-and-account/#plans).

## Quick tips

- **Send the stage link early.** The link stays the same through stop and start, so you can send it out before the show.
- **Stop stages you are not using.** This prevents unwanted access or changes. The link and project are kept.
- **Give viewers a share link, not the stage link.** The stage link opens the editor. A share link is read-only, and you can revoke it.
- **Archive before you edit an approved rundown.** Share the archive for sign-off and keep working on the live stage.
- **Upgrade between shows.** Stages never update on their own, so [upgrade](/ontime-cloud/features/#upgrade-the-ontime-version) when you have time to check the new version.
- **Set up an authenticator app.** Then you never wait for a sign-in email. See [Authenticator app](/ontime-cloud/features/#authenticator-app).

## Quick fixes

### "Subscription required" or "Active subscription required"

If you just completed checkout, wait a few seconds and reload the page. \
Otherwise, check the **Team subscription** card in **Team**. If it shows **Action needed**, an Owner can fix the payment with **Manage Subscription**. See [Subscription statuses](/ontime-cloud/features/#subscription-statuses).

### My stages were stopped

Stages are stopped when a subscription ends or a payment fails for good. Nothing is deleted. Once the subscription is active again, click **Start** on each stage.

### I cannot create a stage or an archive

All the stages or archives in your plan are in use. Stopped stages count too. Delete one you no longer need, or move to a [bigger plan](/ontime-cloud/team-and-account/#plans). \
Creating an archive also needs the stage to be running.

### The stage link does not open

- Check that the stage is running.
- If the stage has a password, make sure people have it.
- If the stage was deleted, its link no longer works. A new stage gets a new link.

### Uploading a project fails

The file must be an Ontime project in `.json` format, not empty, and 2 MB or smaller. The stage must be running.

### Upgrade version is disabled

Stop the stage first. If it shows **Already on latest version**, there is nothing to upgrade.

### I forgot my password, or the sign-in email does not arrive

Use the **One-Time Code** tab and click **Send to email**, then set a new password on the **Account** page. \
If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and the address you signed up with. You can resend after a short wait.

### An invite cannot be accepted

An account can only be in one team. If the email already belongs to another team, ask the Owner to invite a different address. If the invite was revoked, ask for a new one.

### I cannot delete my account

If you are an Owner and the team has an active subscription, cancel it first with **Team** > **Manage Subscription**.

### A share link does not work

- **Share unavailable**: the stage may be stopped, or the share, stage or archive was deleted.
- **This link has expired**: create a new share, or [edit the link](/ontime-cloud/share-links/#change-or-revoke-a-share-link) to change its expiry.
- **Old data**: live shares update about once a minute. An `Offline · last synced …` badge means the stage was briefly unreachable. The view recovers on its own.
