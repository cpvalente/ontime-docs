They follow along in any browser, without an Ontime account and without access to your stage.

## Choose the right share

| You want to… | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Let crew follow the running order on their phones | Rundown share |
| Show a client how the show is tracking | Rundown share |
| Put the whole event on a lobby or green room screen | Overview share |
| Give a producer one link for every room | Overview share |
| Send a client the final rundown for sign-off | Archive share |
| Keep a rundown available after you stop or delete the stage | Archive share |

- **Rundown** and **Overview** shares are **live**: they follow the show as it runs, and need the stage to be running.
- **Archive** shares are **frozen**: they show an [archive](/ontime-cloud/features/#archives) exactly as it was captured, and keep working after the stage is stopped or deleted.

:::tip[Combine them]
Capture an archive before you edit a rundown someone has approved. \
Share the archive for reference, and keep a live share for the people who need to see changes as they happen.
:::

## Share a rundown

<Steps>

1. Start the stage you want to share.

2. Open the stage's **More actions** menu and choose **Create rundown share**. \
   Or go to **Shares**, open the **Rundowns** tab and click **Create Stage Rundown Share**.

3. Give the link a **Share name**. It defaults to the stage title.

4. Optionally, choose a **Link expiry** and tick **Require password**.

5. Click **Create share** and copy the link.

</Steps>

Stages with at least one rundown share show a **Shared** badge on the dashboard.

## Share a timeline of several stages

An overview share is a live, read-only copy of the [Overview](/ontime-cloud/overview/): several running stages side by side, with a wall clock.

<Steps>

1. Start the stages you want to show.

2. Go to **Shares**, open the **Overview** tab and click **Create Overview Share**. \
   Or, on the [Overview page](/ontime-cloud/overview/), pick your stages and click **Share this view**.

3. Choose which stages to include. Leave all unticked to include every running stage, including stages you start later.

4. Name the link, optionally set an expiry and a password, then create the share and copy the link.

</Steps>

## Share an archive

<Steps>

1. If you do not have one yet, create an archive: with the stage running, open **More actions** and choose **Create archive**.

2. Go to **Archives** and click **Share** on the archive.

3. Optionally, tick **Require password** and enter a password.

4. Click **Create share**. The link is copied to your clipboard.

</Steps>

Each archive has one link, with no expiry.

## Change or revoke a share link

Manage every link from the **Shares** page. It has a tab for each type: **Overview**, **Rundowns** and **Archives**.

{/* ![The Shares page, with rundown shares and their links](../../../assets/ontime-cloud/share-list.png) */}

- **Rename** a live link, or change its password and expiry.
- **Change which stages** an overview share includes. The link stays the same.
- **Delete** a live link to revoke it straight away.
- For an archive, choose **Edit share** in its menu on **Archives**. **Reset link** gives it a new link, and **Stop sharing** removes it.

Remember to send the password along with a protected link. See [What viewers see](/ontime-cloud/features/#what-share-viewers-see) for expired, deleted and offline links.

:::note[Live, but not instant]
Live shares update about once a minute. Use them to follow a running order, not as a replacement for second-by-second views like the [Operator](/interface/production/operator/) or a [stage timer](/interface/automated/stage-timer/).
:::
