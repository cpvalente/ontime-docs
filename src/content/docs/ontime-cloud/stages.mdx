A stage is a full Ontime instance, hosted for you. Each stage has its own project, link, password and timezone. \
The link stays the same when you stop and start the stage, so you can send it out before the show and shut the stage down between sessions.

You manage stages from the **Stages** page of the dashboard. Every member of your team can manage stages.

![The Stages page in the Ontime Cloud dashboard, with stage cards and the Create Stage button](../../../assets/ontime-cloud/dashboard-stages.png)

## The life of a stage

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Create** | Reserves a permanent link and starts the stage on the latest Ontime version |
| **Run** | Open the stage and run your show, as you would with local Ontime |
| **Stop** | Takes the stage offline. The link and project are kept |
| **Start** | Brings the stage back on the same link, with the project as you left it |
| **Delete** | Permanently removes the stage, its project and its share links |

## Create a stage

<Steps>

1. On **Stages**, click **Create Stage**.

2. Fill in the form:
   - **Stage name**: how the stage appears in the dashboard
   - **Password (optional)**: if set, people need it to open the stage
   - **Timezone**: pick one from the list, or choose **Enter manually** and type an IANA timezone such as `Europe/Oslo`

3. Click **Create stage**.

</Steps>

The stage starts straight away. When it is ready, the card shows a **Running** badge and the stage link under **Quick Access**. \
To start from a saved show, [upload a project](/ontime-cloud/features/#upload-a-project-to-a-stage) or [create a stage from an archive](/ontime-cloud/features/#create-a-stage-from-an-archive).

If **Create Stage** is disabled, all the stages in your [plan](/ontime-cloud/team-and-account/#plans) are in use. Stopped stages count too.

## Run your show

On a running stage card:

- **Open Ontime** opens the stage in a new tab. Build your rundown and share [views](/interface/) with your crew, as you would locally.
- **View live rundown**, under **Quick Access**, opens a read-only rundown inside the dashboard.
- **More actions** has the project tools: download or upload the project, and create an archive.

{/* ![The live rundown of a running stage in the Ontime Cloud dashboard](../../../assets/ontime-cloud/dashboard-live-rundown.png) */}

Connect [Bitfocus Companion](/additional-notes/companion-module/), the [API](/api/) or the [MCP server](/features/mcp-server/) to the stage link instead of a local address.

## Set a stage password

The stage link is public. Anyone who has it can open the stage, including the editor.

- Set a **stage password** to protect the whole stage. To change or remove it, stop the stage and [edit its settings](/ontime-cloud/features/#stage-settings).
- For people who only need to follow the show, send a [read-only share link](/ontime-cloud/share-links/) instead of the stage link.

See [Sharing flows](/concepts/sharing-flows/) for how access works in Ontime.

## Stop and start a stage

<Steps>

1. On the running stage, click **Stop**, then confirm with **Stop stage**.

2. When you need it again, click **Start**.

</Steps>

A stopped stage is offline for everyone, including viewers of its [live share links](/ontime-cloud/share-links/). \
Stop stages when you are not using them, to prevent unwanted access or changes.

## Save versions of your show

Create an **archive** before a rehearsal, between show days or after the event. An archive is a snapshot of the stage's project that stays in Cloud even after you delete the stage.

With the stage running, open **More actions** and choose **Create archive**. \
You can open, download, [share](/ontime-cloud/share-links/#share-an-archive) or start a new stage from an archive. See [Archives](/ontime-cloud/features/#archives).

## Delete a stage

Only stopped stages can be deleted.

<Steps>

1. Stop the stage.

2. Click **Delete**, then confirm with **Delete stage**.

</Steps>

Deleting is permanent. It removes the stage, its project and its share links, and releases its link. Archives you captured from the stage are kept. \
To keep a copy, [download the project](/ontime-cloud/features/#download-a-project) or create an archive first.
