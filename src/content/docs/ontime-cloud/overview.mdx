The **Overview** puts all your running stages on one timeline with a wall clock. \
Use it when you run several rooms, parallel tracks or a multi-day event, and need to see the whole event at a glance.

{/* ![The Overview page, with several stages on one timeline](../../../assets/ontime-cloud/dashboard-overview.png) */}

## Follow several stages at once

<Steps>

1. Start the stages you want to follow.

2. Open **Overview** in the dashboard.

3. Use **Select stages** to choose which stages to show.

</Steps>

Use the timeline settings to switch between 12h and 24h time and to change the grid interval.

To look at a single stage in more detail, use **View live rundown** on its card on the [Stages](/ontime-cloud/stages/) page.

## Share the Overview

Put the Overview on a lobby screen, or send it to a producer or show caller who needs every room in one place.

On the Overview page, pick your stages and click **Share this view**. This creates a live, read-only [overview share](/ontime-cloud/share-links/#share-a-timeline-of-several-stages) that anyone can open without logging in.

If you share without picking any stages, the link shows every running stage, including stages you start later.

## Good to know

- The Overview only lists stages that are **running**. If a stage cannot be reached, its lane shows **Offline** until it is back.
- The Overview needs an active subscription.
- Like other live shares, overview shares update about once a minute.
